# Blanding 3.0: product spec

## Who it is for

Higher ed marketers who know their copy is bland and need two things: proof they can show their boss, and a way to fix it that does not start with "rewrite the tagline." First audience: attendees of the AMA Higher Ed Symposium session "Your Brand Is Bland. Let's Fix That!" (Tracey Halvorsen, adeo + Jeff Holeman, UF Warrington).

## What we learned from 1.0 and 2.0 (source: Tracey's meeting notes)

- 300+ institutions audited. Median score around 50. Most cluster 50-59. Very few break 80, and the ones that do are narrow and specific (a concrete research program, a great-books college).
- Blanding is a strategy and governance problem, not a writing problem. Copy reads generic because too many stakeholders approve it.
- **Distinction, not differentiation.** Everyone grants degrees. The job is to find what is genuinely only yours.
- **Promise needs proof on the same page.** "World-class faculty" is a promise. A named professor doing a named thing is proof.
- **The logo-swap test.** If the copy reads fine with a rival's name on it, it is not yours.
- **Distinction lives in the detail, not the tagline.** Ladder up from the specific to the platform.
- Tracey's direction for this version (Oct 27): "Trying to do too much, making mistakes. Keep it simple and fun. A light diagnostic, not a scientific tool."
- Tone reference: the Halloween horror-trailer series. "The Sixth Sentence: I see dead copy."

## So 3.0 is sharper, not bigger

Runs entirely in the browser. Paste copy, get a diagnosis. No crawler, no API keys, no accounts. Works offline, which matters because conference Wi-Fi is a coin flip.

### The five instruments

1. **Distinction Score** (0-100, higher is better) with a band: Embalmed, Haunted, Twitching, Breathing, Unmistakable. One-line verdict.
2. **The Autopsy.** Your copy, sentence by sentence. Clichés highlighted. Each one, on click: what it actually says ("vibrant community" means "there are people here") and the fix ("name the club, the night, the headcount").
3. **The Logo Swap.** One toggle replaces your name with "Literally Any University." Sentences that survive are greyed as "could be anyone's." Usually most of them. That is the joke and the point.
4. **Promise vs. Proof ledger.** Every promise, and whether anything nearby backs it up. Orphaned promises flagged.
5. **Three Things Only You Can Say.** The AMA takeaway exercise. Type three claims. Each gets checked live: specific? provable? would a rival choke on it? Then ladder up.

Plus:
- **Rival Séance** (optional): paste up to three competitors' copy and see the phrases you share. You are not competing with them. You are harmonizing with them.
- **The Poster**: a downloadable B-movie poster of your diagnosis. Title, tagline, score. It is the thing people screenshot and send to their VP.

## Non-goals for 3.0

URL crawling, accounts, leaderboards, program-level tracking over time (2.0 has those, server side). Any AI rewriting of copy: AI is the spotter, not the writer.
