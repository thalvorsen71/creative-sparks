# Corpus provenance

`corpus.json` is generated by `python3 -I src/corpus/build-corpus.py` (stdlib only, deterministic, no network; two runs give byte-identical output). Source repo: `/home/user/blanding-t0` (Tracey's Blanding 2.0 / T0 study), read-only.

## Sources
- `reports/agreement_ring150.json`: per school `agreed_promises` (theme `type`, `support`, `readers`, `quotes`). Agreed means both readers (Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.6, temp 0) voted the theme.
- `reports/ring150/proof_kind.json`: evidence spans with a `kind` (Haiku-labelled) and `details` (the "only you can say this" specifics, with click `depth`).
- `reports/wide25/proof_kind.json` and `HANDOFF.md`: cross-check for proof location.

## Counts and how computed
- Sample: 150 schools with at least one agreed promise. The file holds 151 school keys; `barnard.edu` has no agreed promise and is excluded. Sample = wide25 + 15 lowest + 15 highest + 95 seeded random (seed 20261026) out of 562 captured. Not a pure random sample.
- Theme share = schools with an agreed promise in the theme / 150. A theme counts once per school.
- Support = shares over 347 agreed promises: backed 271, claim_only 37, split 39.
- Proof kinds = 582 evidence spans across the 150 schools (both readers' spans, so a span both readers cite counts twice). Kinds use T0 names. Each also carries `engineKind`, the Amendment 2 engine value: operational_practice=operating_mechanism, documented_outcome=reported_outcome, funded_commitment=published_policy, documented_experience=example_or_story, restated_claim and third_party_recognition unchanged. announced_intention has no engine equivalent (null). The mapping is approximate.
- Proof location = `details` depth over ring150: 11 homepage, 160 one click, 68 two clicks, of 239. The wide25 file gives 3 / 38 / 16 of 57, identical to HANDOFF.md; it is kept in `proofLocation.wide25`. The contract's top-level numbers use ring150 (the same sample as everything else); say so if you quote the HANDOFF figures.
- `generated` is the latest dated session heading in HANDOFF.md (not today's date), so the build is reproducible.

## Examples
Up to 5 per theme from agreed promises with `readers == 2`. Quotes are split into sentences (verbatim substrings). A sentence is kept if it has 6 to 30 words, starts with a capital and ends with terminal punctuation, and has no digits, URLs, dashes, colons, semicolons, number words, all-caps tokens, or capitalised words after the first word (proper-noun proxy). It must contain a common function or verb word (fragment filter), must not start "The average/median/total", and must not contain the school's domain stem or any 4+ letter word from its page titles. Candidates are sorted by word count then text, one per school.

## Caveats
- Themes with too few clean generic sentences get fewer than 5 examples: Career outcomes (4), Tradition (3), Innovation and Rigor (2 each), Prestige, Place and Creativity (0).
- Names are filtered heuristically; a human skim before public use is wise.
- Quotes keep the site's own typography (curly apostrophes etc.).
- Proof kinds were labelled by Haiku, post hoc; the 2-reader agreement applies to themes and support, not to kinds.
- Reader audit stats: misquotes 11/1624 (Sonnet), 5/1577 (Opus) per the T0 agreement meta.
- 2026-10-31: cut 'men of distinction' example (identifies an all-male college). Tracey's call. EXCLUDE list in build-corpus.py.
